Unlock this phone's region?

The stock region service will be updated. Root-manager approval is required, and the phone must reboot before the new state is visible.

Lock this phone's region?

This requests stock locked state 1. After reboot, SIM service may be restricted by the active OPlus region policy.

Confirm the consequences

Locking is a real modem-state change, not a visual app setting. You may need this unlock tool and another reboot to restore state 0.
